## 2. The moat: the three-layer brain

This is what no competitor has, and what is genuinely hard to copy because it's
*domain reasoning*, not plumbing.

### Layer 1 - HOW to buy it (`detectStrategy`)
For every lead, Veori scores **all five acquisition strategies** and ranks them:

- **Cash / wholesale** (the default, untouched)
- **Subject-to** (take over the existing mortgage)
- **Seller finance** (owner carries the note)
- **Lease-option** (control now, buy later)
- **Novation** (re-paper and resell near retail)

It returns a *ranked* list with a confidence score and a reason, then an operator
can **manually override** it. The chosen strategy is the single source of truth the
voice AI and the offer math both read - so the pitch and the numbers never disagree.

> **Competitor reality:** GHL, REISift, Launch Control have **zero** concept of an
> acquisition strategy. A lead is a lead is a row. They cannot tell a user that *this*
> seller is a subject-to and *that* one is a novation - because they don't know what
> those words mean.

### Layer 2 - WHAT the numbers are (`computeStrategyOffer` / `creativeFinanceCalc`)
Per-strategy offer math, including guardrails a rookie misses:

- Subject-to **upside-down guard**: if the loan ≥ ARV, there's no equity to pay -
  Veori refuses to fabricate a seller payout and reframes the offer as pure relief.
- Seller-finance amortized P&I, down %, term, optional balloon.
- Lease-option price/fee/term. Novation spread after reno + agent + closing.
- Cash stays exactly the proven MAO math (`ARV × 0.70 − repairs`).

> **Competitor reality:** Calculators exist as *separate* spreadsheets/tools. None
> are *wired into the live conversation*. None refuse a bad number on the operator's behalf.

### Layer 3 - the VETERAN READ + negotiation chess (`dealIntelligence` + `negotiationPlaybook`)
This is the layer that makes a 20-year veteran say *"this is better than me."*

- **Seller psychology read** - a 0–100 motivation score with the *human drivers*
  named (foreclosure clock, tax lien, inherited burden, landlord fatigue, life event).
- **Leverage posture** - buyer / seller / balanced, computed from distress + equity +
  whether they're already listed. It tells the AI *who holds the cards* before it dials in.
- **Deal grade A/B/C/D** - a go/no-go verdict so the operator never burns a call on
  junk and always knows how hard to push.
- **Negotiation playbook** - per-strategy *chess*: where to anchor, what to trade
  concessions for (never for free), when to **walk**, and the one line that wins that
  kind of deal - all modulated by the leverage posture, all kept strictly inside the
  honesty / no-pressure / DNC rules.
- **Market & legal reality** baked into each strategy: due-on-sale truth for
  subject-to, the high-rate environment edge for seller-finance, equity traps for
  lease-option, spread/disclosure discipline for novation.

> **Competitor reality:** This layer simply **does not exist anywhere in the
> category.** The closest anyone gets is a static script template. Veori reasons about
> the specific human on the other end of the line, every time.

## 4. The "need in the market" we uniquely fill

1. **The skill gap is the real bottleneck, not the lead count.** Every tool floods
   the operator with leads and dialers. The thing that actually loses deals is a rep
   who doesn't know *how* to structure or *how* to negotiate. Veori puts a master
   closer's judgment into every single conversation - instantly, for a brand-new

2. **Creative finance is where the money is and where the tools are blindest.** In a

   was built creative-first.

3. **Effortless execution.** The operator doesn't assemble a script, run a
   calculator, and guess the seller's mindset. Veori does all three and *makes the

## 5. Why it can't be easily copied

- **It's reasoning, not plumbing.** Anyone can add an email tab. Encoding five
  strategies, their math guardrails, seller psychology, leverage, and per-strategy
  negotiation chess - correctly, safely, and wired into a live voice - is a domain
  problem most teams can't even spec.
- **Compounding intelligence.** Call outcomes feed the brain; the read sharpens over
  time. A CRM's row doesn't get smarter. Veori's read does.
- **Safety as a feature, not a bolt-on.** The brain negotiates hard *inside* hard
  honesty rules (AI disclosure, DNC, recording consent, no pressure/deception). That
  earns trust competitors trade away with spammy blasts.
